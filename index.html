What We Do

Backflow testing, repair, and the paperwork that follows

One tester for the whole job — the test itself, the repair if the device fails, the retest, and the documentation your municipality expects.

Annual Backflow Testing

Backflow prevention assemblies need testing on a set schedule to confirm they still hold. We complete the required test on every device at the property and produce the documentation that goes with it.

Backflow Repair

Fouled check valves, worn relief valves, and perished seals are the usual causes of a failed test. We troubleshoot the assembly and repair it rather than defaulting to a full replacement.

Retesting & Certification

A device that fails has to pass before it is compliant. Where we repair on site, we retest on site, so the visit ends with a working assembly and a completed report.

Report Submission

Reporting requirements vary by municipality. Eagle Eye submits the required test documentation through the applicable municipal reporting system on your behalf. You do not need to learn how your city's system works.

Commercial Backflow Service

Restaurants, retail, strata corporations, office and industrial buildings, and multi-family properties — including sites with several assemblies that need testing and documenting individually.

Residential Backflow Service

Qualifying residential assemblies and irrigation systems, tested and documented the same way. Irrigation is tested once the system is charged for the season.

Pricing

Straightforward backflow testing pricing

Every installation is slightly different — the number of assemblies, their size and type, and how easy they are to reach all move the number. Rather than publish a figure that turns out not to apply to you, we quote on the actual device.

  • Send a photo of the device, your municipal notice, or a previous test report.
  • We come back with clear pricing before any work begins.
  • If the device fails, you hear the repair cost before we proceed.
  • You do not pay until the work is complete.
