Services

Backflow Services Without the Runaround

Testing, repairs, documentation, and submission — one company handling the full process, so you are not chasing a tester, a plumber, and a city form separately.

Annual Backflow Testing

The required test on every assembly at the property, carried out by a certified tester and fully documented.

Backflow Repairs

Fouled checks, worn relief valves, and perished seals troubleshot and repaired, including minor parts replacement.

Retesting & Certification

A device that fails has to pass before it is compliant. Where we repair on site, we retest on site.

Report Submission

Reporting requirements vary by municipality. Eagle Eye submits the required test documentation through the applicable municipal reporting system on your behalf.

Commercial & Strata

Restaurants, retail, offices, industrial and multi-family buildings — including sites with several assemblies to document individually.

Residential

Qualifying residential assemblies and irrigation systems, tested and documented the same way.
