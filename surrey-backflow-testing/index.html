Backflow testing in Surrey

Surrey is geographically enormous and it does not behave like one city. Whalley and City Centre are densifying fast, with new towers going up and new assemblies going in with them. Newton and Guildford are dominated by retail and older commercial strip development. Cloverdale and the eastern edge are still substantially rural, on acreage with irrigation.

That spread matters when you are booking. A tester who works mostly in one corner of the Lower Mainland will treat South Surrey as a long trip. Eagle Eye covers the city as a whole, and for property managers with buildings in more than one part of Surrey, testing can normally be grouped so the whole portfolio is handled together.

What a test includes

  • Certified test of each backflow assembly on site
  • Repair of many common failures during the same visit
  • Retest after any repair
  • Test documentation completed and submitted for you
  • A reminder when the next test is due

No payment until the work is complete.

How testing works in Surrey

  1. Send us the property

    Call, text, or send the booking form with the address and, if you have it, a photo of the device or your municipal notice.

  2. We test the assembly

    A certified tester attends and completes the required test on every backflow assembly at the address.

  3. Repairs, if the device fails

    Newer Surrey construction tends to fail in predictable ways: debris from commissioning fouling a check valve, or a relief valve that has never seated properly since installation. Both are usually serviceable on the spot. On irrigation assemblies that have sat through a winter, freeze damage is the more common finding, and that sometimes needs a part we order in.

  4. We file the paperwork

    Reporting requirements vary by municipality. Eagle Eye submits the required test documentation through the applicable municipal reporting system on your behalf.

Local considerations

Because Surrey covers so much ground, scheduling is where the time is won or lost. If you manage several buildings across Whalley, Newton, and South Surrey, tell us all of the addresses at once rather than booking them one at a time — grouping the route usually means fewer visits, less coordination on your end, and a faster turnaround on the whole set.

Questions

Surrey backflow testing questions

Do you cover all of Surrey, including South Surrey and Cloverdale?

Yes — Whalley, City Centre, Guildford, Newton, Fleetwood, Cloverdale, and South Surrey. The whole city is in our regular service area.

I manage buildings in several parts of Surrey. Can they be done together?

Yes, and it is worth asking for. Send the full address list and we will group the testing into as few visits as the geography allows.

My irrigation system failed after the winter. Can it be repaired?

Often, yes. Freeze damage ranges from a cracked relief valve to a split body. The first is usually a same-visit repair; the second may need a part ordered in. We will tell you which it is at the time of the test.

Does a townhome complex need testing?

If the complex has a backflow assembly protecting its domestic or irrigation service, it generally does. Strata-managed complexes usually hold this at the corporation level rather than with individual owners.
