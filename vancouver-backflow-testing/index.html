Backflow testing in Vancouver

Vancouver has one of the densest concentrations of backflow assemblies in the province, and very little of it sits in an easy location. Downtown and West End towers keep their assemblies in below-grade parkade mechanical rooms. Restaurants along Main, Commercial, Denman, and through Gastown often have devices tucked behind kitchen equipment or in shared service corridors.

Older buildings through Strathcona, Mount Pleasant, and Kitsilano add a further wrinkle: assemblies retrofitted into buildings that were never designed around them, sometimes in a crawlspace or a closet that nobody has opened in a year. Eagle Eye is used to finding them. If you are not sure where your device is or whether you even have one, send us the address and we will work it out.

What a test includes

  • Certified test of each backflow assembly on site
  • Repair of many common failures during the same visit
  • Retest after any repair
  • Test documentation completed and submitted for you
  • A reminder when the next test is due

No payment until the work is complete.

How testing works in Vancouver

  1. Send us the property

    Call, text, or send the booking form with the address and, if you have it, a photo of the device or your municipal notice.

  2. We test the assembly

    A certified tester attends and completes the required test on every backflow assembly at the address.

  3. Repairs, if the device fails

    Downtown buildings are exactly where a second appointment hurts most, because coordinating access twice can take longer than the repair. We carry common replacement parts and rebuild kits, so where a device fails on a serviceable fault we will usually repair and retest it before we leave.

  4. We file the paperwork

    Reporting requirements vary by municipality. Eagle Eye submits the required test documentation through the applicable municipal reporting system on your behalf.

Local considerations

Access is the single biggest cause of a rescheduled test in Vancouver. Parkade mechanical rooms, locked service corridors, and restaurants that can only give access outside service hours all need arranging in advance. Tell us up front what the access situation is and we will book a window that works — for most buildings you do not need to be there yourself, as long as we can get in.

Questions

Vancouver backflow testing questions

Do I need to be on site for the test in Vancouver?

Usually not. What we need is access. If a building manager, concierge, or caretaker can let us into the mechanical room, most tests are completed without the owner or strata council present.

Can you test outside restaurant service hours?

In most cases, yes. Tell us your service window when you book and we will schedule around it wherever the building allows access at that time.

My building has several assemblies. Is that one visit?

Normally yes. Multiple devices at one address are tested in a single visit, and each one is documented and submitted separately as required.

The water will be off during the test?

Briefly, to the line the device protects — usually a matter of minutes per assembly. For restaurants and medical offices we will confirm the timing with you before we shut anything down.
